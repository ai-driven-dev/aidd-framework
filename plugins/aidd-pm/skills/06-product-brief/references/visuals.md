# Product Views

| View | Use for | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any view | structure clearer than prose | derived from the brief, under `Product View`, no new claim, replaces redundant prose |
| Compact table | alternatives, groups, claims, or signals | comparison only |
| Mermaid | sequences, relationships, boundaries, or opportunities | small, directional, and valid |
| ASCII wireframe | a concrete product screen | structure only; no styling or final copy |
